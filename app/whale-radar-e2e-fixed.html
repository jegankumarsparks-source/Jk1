How this radar makes a signal

These are the exact stages behind the counters and activity feed. A crowded discovery feed does not imply there are tradable signals. It means candidates are being checked. The bar is deliberately stricter for a whale alert.

01

Candidate intake

Recent token profiles and recently or top-boosted tokens bring token addresses into the queue. A sampled pair search adds recently created pairs returned by public search results. This is a sampled set, not every new Solana token or pair. Duplicates are removed by mint. If one feed fails, others may still run; each failure appears in Source health and Activity log.

02

Pair and liquidity check

Every token address in the queue is checked against Dexscreener token pairs. The most liquid Solana pair is used for the current price and summary. Tokens without a positive price or at least $10,000 reported USD liquidity are not admitted to the watched-coin database. A failed pair request is an error, not an empty market.

03

Early-holder snapshot

For a watched token under its observed 2x threshold, Solana getTokenLargestAccounts returns up to twenty largest token accounts. Token accounts are resolved to wallet owners. Program-owned or executable owner accounts, known programs, token mint and freeze authorities, and owners dominating more than 40% of those top balances are excluded. A wallet that appears after a coin has already doubled is not treated as an early winner.

04

Observed 2x promotion

Only an owner captured at the early-holder snapshot can be promoted when the currently observed token price reaches at least two times its first observed price. That comparison is based on the app’s local price observations, not on launch price or any historical low. Promotion is a heuristic: it can include passive holders or unknown exchange owners, and it does not prove profitable buying or selling.

05

Wallet baseline

When the app first finds a promoted wallet, it records the newest confirmed signature as its baseline. Older transactions are not interpreted as fresh acquisitions. The app polls up to four wallets per cycle and reads up to forty recent signatures for each. If more signatures arrive between polls than fit in the request, older activity can be missed. The activity feed warns when a backlog fills that window.

06

Acquisition evidence

For each new confirmed transaction, the app compares the wallet owner’s pre- and post-token balances using raw integer units. A strictly positive difference is an acquisition candidate, excluding wrapped SOL. It checks a current priced, sufficiently liquid Solana pair before making a signal. This proves only an increase in the wallet’s token balance, not the economic reason: it could be a purchase, transfer, airdrop, or other transaction.

07

One coin, multiple wallets

The first verified acquisition for a mint creates one signal card with its source signature and the first wallet. Later verified acquisitions by distinct promoted wallets add those wallets to the same card, with a multi-wallet visual highlight. Reprocessing the same signature or wallet does not make another signal or another paper entry. The visible card links out for independent verification and optional manual trading.

08

Paper position

With the paper bot on, a first signal opens one $10 virtual position if at least $10 cash is available and fewer than ten positions are open. Starting cash is $200. A second wallet on the same mint changes the signal’s overlap count but does not add a second position. The bot never signs a transaction, accesses a real wallet, or places a trade.

09

Exit and price gap

Signal and paper prices are refreshed from Dexscreener while the browser is active. A position closes fully when the first observed price reaches at least 130% or at most 70% of entry. The recorded exit uses the observed price, so a gap past the threshold leads to a different result from exactly +30% or -30%. It is a simulation, not an executable quote, and does not include slippage, tax, swap fees, or failed execution.

10

Audit warnings

Mint and freeze authority checks read current Solana mint account data where available. UNKNOWN means the request did not provide a clear answer; it is not OFF. These warnings are display-only. They cannot identify every exploit, concentrated holder, mint extension, pool issue, or malicious developer action. A green badge is not a safety guarantee.

11

Pauses and gaps

The engine runs only in an open, visible browser tab. Mobile operating systems may suspend the tab even when it seems open. The next cycle resumes when the tab is foregrounded. The last completed cycle and latest source outcomes show whether new data arrived. Local storage holds the candidate database and paper history; clearing browser storage can remove both.

12

Read a live count

Coins scanned counts token addresses whose pair checks were attempted; it can count repeat checks. Holders pulled counts nonzero token accounts returned from top-holder snapshots, not unique humans. Whales discovered counts newly promoted wallet owners. Signatures parsed counts successfully retrieved confirmed transactions after a wallet’s baseline. These are work counters, not success rates or returns.

13

Read the time buckets

The Signals tab partitions by the signal’s local detection time: within one hour, older than one and within five hours, older than five and within twenty-four hours, or all locally stored signals. A detection time is not necessarily a market entry time. The scanner’s time and log events use the device clock; inaccurate device time can make time buckets misleading.

14

Tap-to-buy route

The primary Dexscreener link opens the token page for the mint. GMGN, Jupiter, and Birdeye links are alternate research and manual swap routes. These are external destinations; the app does not control their current liquidity, quotes, contract safety or availability. Verify the mint and price yourself before any real trade. Tapping a link does not touch the virtual paper balance.

15

Backup and reset

An export saves the browser’s current state as a local JSON file. Restore replaces all current local scanner, signals and paper data after confirmation. Keep backups private: they contain watched wallet addresses and activity. The separate activity export holds the bounded recent log. Clearing discovery keeps paper history, and open paper positions continue to be repriced from their stored mints.

16

Failure conditions

A public endpoint can respond with HTTP errors, a rate limit, an RPC service error, malformed data or browser cross-origin restrictions. The engine logs the failing stage and continues independent stages. It never converts a missing response into a synthetic wallet or a signal. Public RPC is throttled and requests may take multiple cycles as the watched set grows; there is no promise of complete real-time coverage.

Research tool, not advice. Token prices, holder identities and on-chain transactions require independent review before any financial decision.

What a signal is not

A token entering the watch database is not a signal. A wallet rising to the proven list is not a signal. A 2x coin does not mean that the wallet bought at the earliest observed price, sold at the top, or made money. The only signal event is a new positive net token balance observed for an already promoted wallet in a confirmed transaction, combined with a current priced and liquid market pair.

External chart or swap links are for you to inspect and decide. The paper lab operates independently of those sites and does not model a real filled order. No private keys, signing permissions or real balances are required.

Why zero signals is sometimes correct

On a new installation the radar first has to collect token addresses, take snapshots while a candidate is still below 2x, observe a later doubling, resolve the saved owner wallets, set a baseline on each promoted wallet, and then encounter a later confirmed token acquisition. Missing any step leaves no legitimate signal. The scanner shows which step is active and which source returned an error so a zero alert count does not hide a failure.

The app cannot backfill historic 2x winners reliably from the current public feeds. Public APIs may not show all pairs and wallets. Leaving a browser open is not equivalent to a server-side always-on monitor, especially on a phone. The number of tracked coins grows with observed source coverage, not by inventing rows.

Operating guide

Keep the Scanner tab open to follow a whole cycle. When the engine is working, the pulsing ring indicates that the page is processing. The current target identifies the mint or wallet at the active stage. The five stage blocks show intake, pair checks, holder pulls, wallet polls and price refreshes. Once a cycle finishes, the label changes to a completed or partial state; the next cycle begins while the page remains visible.

Use the Discovery feed to browse observed candidates. Holder snapshot means the top token accounts were captured before a price doubling. The 2x watch filter surfaces coins nearing or exceeding that benchmark, and the green PROVEN tag means at least one eligible owner was promoted. Swipe down to inspect the activity ledger; its most recent event is first. The event list is bounded so storage stays manageable, and the downloadable activity log captures the currently retained entries rather than a permanent full audit archive.

Signals are separated from the candidate feed. The default window covers only alerts detected in the last hour. Move to 1-5h, 5-24h or All if that window is empty. A signal card has the original source transaction link, token address, current market snapshot and the number of independently observed promoted wallets. If the same coin is acquired by two promoted wallets it is highlighted as an overlap, not duplicated. If a market source returns an error, a stale saved signal may still appear with its last locally observed price; inspect timestamps and source health.

In Paper, cash falls by $10 when a new valid alert opens a virtual position. Equity includes currently valued positions and will depend on the most recently observed price. A position exits when the refresh first observes either exit threshold. Closed history reports realized results only. Turning the bot off prevents new entries; it does not erase or freeze open positions. Use Reset paper bank only if you intend to discard the old virtual record.

Settings contains the most recent cycle status, response health for each source that has answered, a manual run button, backup and restore controls. A manual cycle is ignored when one is already in progress. Restore replaces the browser-local database. If a phone suspends the page, returning to the tab starts another cycle. A persistent warning means the public API or browser is not supplying reliable data; retry later or use another network rather than treating silence as proof that no whales are active.

Data quality checklist

Before treating a card as actionable, first check that the timestamp is recent and that the activity log has successful market and RPC responses since the last pause. Then inspect the source transaction for the listed wallet and token. A positive token-balance delta does not prove that SOL or a stablecoin was spent. Review transfer instructions and counterparties yourself to distinguish a swap from a transfer or airdrop. Look at the mint address, not only the symbol, since unrelated tokens can have the same symbol.

Check the current market depth, price impact and holder concentration on the external chart or swap site. The liquidity figure in a saved card is whatever the source most recently reported, and it may already have changed. A holder that was early on one 2x coin is not necessarily informed about another token. A shared owner between multiple token accounts is deduplicated during holder collection, but one person can still control multiple wallet addresses. The multi-wallet badge means distinct addresses, not independently verified people.

Authority checks do not replace a security audit. The screen intentionally says UNKNOWN when the necessary field is missing or the request has not succeeded. It also does not detect every malicious token extension, freeze condition, wallet blacklisting rule, honeypot, trading tax or liquidity withdrawal. Do not treat the paper bot’s profit line as evidence that an actual swap would be filled at those prices or within those time intervals.

Source and method notes

Dexscreener public profiles, boosts and pair search feed candidate discovery and prices. RugCheck public token reports supply top-holder snapshots. Solana public RPC endpoints (PublicNode first, Solana Foundation and OnFinality as fallback) supply getMultipleAccounts, getSignaturesForAddress and getTransaction wallet evidence. Every signal stores its source signature. Browser-local storage can be cleared by the device.
